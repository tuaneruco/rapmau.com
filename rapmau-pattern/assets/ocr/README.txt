Self-hosted OCR assets (loaded on demand):
Tesseract.js 7.0.0 and tesseract.js-core 7.0.0 — Apache-2.0, licenses included.
English LSTM language data: @tesseract.js-data/eng 1.0.0, 4.0.0_best_int — Apache-2.0.
Source: https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js and https://github.com/naptha/tessdata
No images are sent to a remote OCR service. The three LSTM cores provide
non-SIMD, SIMD, and relaxed-SIMD compatibility. OEM 1 is required.
